SLAMDUNK - TRAIT PLAYER DRAFT v0.1
==================================

Purpose
-------
Balance sample for a future five-level Trait system. This is a design draft,
not production seed data.

Trait Total = sum of all assigned Trait levels.

Suggested Trait Total ranges with 27 available Traits:

Base       :  0-8
Common     :  6-14
Uncommon   : 12-22
Alpha      : 20-34
All-Star   : 30-45
Superstar  : 45-60
GOAT       : 58-75

Adjacent ranges intentionally overlap. Rarity is determined by the complete
card (Stats, Traits, role and matchup value), not Trait Total alone.

Display-name changes used in this draft:

Mamba Instinct replaces Tough Shot Maker.
Clutch Gene replaces Clutch Performer.
Moment Saver replaces Clutch Defender.

Review Notes
------------

1. Tier 5 represents a defining or historically elite skill, not merely a high
   rarity bonus.
2. A GOAT does not receive all 27 Traits. Weaknesses and archetype identity
   must remain visible.
3. Superstar and GOAT ranges overlap because a specialized Superstar can have
   more Trait levels than a specialized GOAT center, while the complete GOAT
   card can still be stronger through Stats and more impactful Trait choices.
4. Direct probability modifiers must retain the shared Battle Engine clamp;
   a larger Trait Total mainly expands contextual coverage rather than allowing
   unlimited stacking on one action.
